# Changelog

All notable changes to Motitech Front are documented here.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-25

First public version of the personal portfolio.

### Added

- Bilingual interface in English and Spanish, with the choice saved in the browser.
- Home, about, hire, projects, contact, terms, and privacy pages.
- Project descriptions for airline platforms, mortgage loan origination, and insurance, including Azure, AWS, SQL Server, and microservices.
- AI-assisted delivery with Cursor, Claude Code, and Spec-Driven Development, shown in the technology list and the working method.
- Contact email stays closed until the client accepts that way of working and the terms.
- Experience with support and maintenance of legacy systems, shown in the profile and in hire.
- Security headers for the production build, and a closed list of allowed interface languages.
- Contact form that delivers messages to the portfolio inbox through Formspree, with a local delivery log that omits the message body.
- "Last updated" labels and the footer year follow the current date instead of a fixed year.
- Page titles, descriptions, and share tags for search engines and link previews.
- Freelance terms covering cancellation, client delays, AI tools, and a liability cap at the fees paid.
- Responsive layout for phone and desktop widths.
- Unit tests for translations, navigation, language switching, and the main pages.
